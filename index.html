4. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. The Application does not require user account registration, profile creation, social logins, or personal identification details (such as your legal name, physical address, phone number, or government-issued identifiers).

No Account Required: You can practice conversational English, minimal pairs, and phoneme articulation immediately without creating an account or providing an email address.

The categories of data processed during app usage are:

  • Ephemeral Voice / Acoustic Input: Audio captured through your device microphone when you deliberately engage voice recording to practice speech.
  • Educational Practice Records: Local practice metrics such as words per minute (WPM), articulation score percentages, streak days, and completed dialogue turns.
  • Diagnostics and Technical Metadata: Operating system level, device model, and standard application state data managed locally on your device.

5. Microphone Permission and Voice Recordings

The Application requests access to your device microphone via the android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO runtime permission. We strictly uphold the following principles:

  • Explicit User Action Only: The microphone is never activated in the background or without your explicit touch interaction (e.g., tapping the dedicated 72dp voice recording button).
  • No Continuous Listening: Audio sampling begins when you tap to record and stops immediately when you release or complete the turn.
  • No Audio Archiving: Raw recorded voice audio clips are processed dynamically for speech analysis and are not permanently archived, stored, sold, or built into biometric voice profiles.

6. Speech Recognition and Audio Processing

The Application analyzes user pronunciation and spoken English clarity through on-device and Android platform speech evaluation pipelines. Spoken text is compared with target International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) standards, stress markers, and pacing thresholds.

Audio playback from the AI Coach Alex is synthesized locally on your Android device using the built-in Android TextToSpeech (TTS) framework, eliminating the need to transmit speech audio to external audio-generation servers for coaching prompts.

7. AI-Powered Features and Educational Feedback

English Coach AI provides intelligent language learning assistance, conversational simulations (such as Job Interviews, Casual Ordering, and Project Debriefs), and grammar guidance.

Where AI features interface with cloud-based intelligence services (such as Google Gemini API or Firebase AI Logic):

  • Transcripts or exercise text prompts required to generate educational replies are processed strictly for real-time educational evaluation.
  • Prompts are processed in accordance with Google's API Privacy and Developer Terms.
  • User prompts and transcripts are not sold to third parties or used for external commercial advertising targeting.

8. Personal Mistake Intelligence

To help you conquer difficult sounds (such as dental fricatives /θ/ and /ð/, near-open vowels /æ/, and minimal pair contrasts), the Application flags words that require review.

This mistake intelligence operates exclusively as educational tracking metrics within the active app instance to customize recommended drill exercises (e.g., high-priority phoneme reviews in the Phoneme Lab).

9. Learning Progress and Practice History

Your educational metrics—including your daily target completion (e.g., 12 of 15 minutes), calculated CEFR grade level (e.g., B2 Upper Intermediate), active streaks, and conversational turn history—are stored locally on your device.

Clearing application storage or uninstalling the Application removes these stored local progress metrics.

10. Device Information and Technical Data

Like standard Android applications, technical variables may be processed in memory during runtime to ensure proper layout rendering, audio hardware synchronization, and orientation handling across phones, foldables, and tablets.

We do not collect persistent hardware identifiers such as IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, or SIM serial numbers.

11. Internet and Network Access

The Application declares the android.permission.INTERNET permission in its manifest. This permission is used strictly to communicate with backend APIs for cloud educational intelligence, verify service availability, and maintain active conversational responses.

13. Data Storage and Security

We enforce industry-standard security safeguards to protect your information:

  • On-Device Isolation: Application preferences and practice metrics are saved in protected app sandboxes using standard Android OS sandboxing mechanisms.
  • Encrypted Transport: All network requests made by the app are transmitted over secure HTTPS (TLS encryption) channels.
  • No Unsecured Storage: We do not store sensitive audio or transcripts on unsecured external public media directories.

14. Data Retention

Because the app does not maintain user account registries on remote databases:

  • Locally saved progress, WPM statistics, and phoneme logs persist on your physical device until you clear app cache/data or uninstall the app.
  • Voice audio captured during speaking turns is ephemeral and is not retained after pronunciation analysis is rendered.

15. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Bhushan-Codex-Studio does not sell, rent, monetize, or lease your personal data or voice recordings to any third party or data broker.

Data may only be disclosed if required by enforceable laws, valid legal processes, or government requests, or to protect the vital rights, safety, and property of our users and the public.

16. Children's Privacy

The Application is designed as a general-audience educational speech and language improvement tool. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age (or the applicable minimum age under local jurisdiction law).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to us without appropriate consent, please contact us immediately at bhushancodexstudio@gmail.com, and we will take prompt steps to verify and delete such information.
